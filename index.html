Clipper

A personal utility for publishing short video clips.

What it is

Clipper is a single-operator tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It prepares short vertical video clips and publishes them to social accounts that the operator personally owns and controls.

Who can use it

Nobody else. It is not a product, not a service, and has no users beyond its author. It is not distributed, sold, or offered to the public. It authenticates to exactly one account: its operator's own.

What it does with Google APIs

It uses the YouTube Data API for one purpose: uploading a video file to the operator's own YouTube channel. It requests a single scope, youtube.upload, which permits uploading and nothing else. It cannot read the channel, its analytics, its comments, or its subscribers.

Data

It collects no data from anyone. It has no visitors, no accounts, no database, and no analytics. See the privacy policy.
